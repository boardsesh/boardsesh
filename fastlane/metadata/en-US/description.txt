Climb on Kilter, Tension, MoonBoard and more, plus your Spraywall. Keep your climbs, shared queue and logbook in one app.

YOUR BOARDS. ONE APP.
Boardsesh works with Kilter, Tension, MoonBoard, Woods, Decoy, Touchstone, Grasshopper and So iLL. Switch between supported boards and keep your climbing history together.

Your Spraywall, too
A home Spraywall, or a Spraywall at your gym? Photograph it and Boardsesh suggests holds from the photo. Fix what it missed, then set climbs, queue them and log sends in the same logbook as your other boards. No LEDs needed: the holds are highlighted on your photo. Share the Spraywall by link and your crew can set on it too. Stripped the Spraywall? Reset it with a new photo, and the old Spraywall is archived with its climbs and sends. Swap a single hold and any climb that used it is flagged.

Light your Spraywall
Have a WLED controller and LEDs? Map them on your Spraywall photo, test holds and save the setup for your crew. Find WLED on your Wi-Fi, set brightness and choose an automatic switch-off time for Wi-Fi lighting. Compatible controllers with Boardsesh Bluetooth support can light climbs over Bluetooth; installing that support is an opt-in beta for matching controllers.

BOARD HISTORY
Share a board and you lose track of the last climb fast, and someone always wipes it by accident.
See climbs lit through Boardsesh on that board: the climb name, who lit it, who set it, grade and angle, and recent sends.

SESSIONS WITH YOUR CREW
Start a session and you and your crew can share one queue everyone can drive. Anyone can light the next climb on the wall. When you're done you get a recap and tracking: sends and attempts, grade spread, your hardest send, and progress over time.

CONTROL FROM YOUR LOCK SCREEN (iOS 17+)
iOS Live Activities tell you what climb is on from your lock screen, and allow you to change it without leaving your lock screen.

BUILD A WORKOUT
Combine warm-ups with Volume, Pyramid, Ladder, Grade Focus, 4x4, Limit Bouldering or On the Minute. Set your grades and rests, preview the climbs and work through them on the wall. Pyramids can repeat the same climbs on the way down. Choose countdown buzzes and Lock Screen rest countdowns in Settings > Timers.

TRACK YOUR CLIMBING
Record sends, flashes and attempts, get a recap with duration, grade spread and your hardest send, and watch your sends and grades climb on the Progress tab.

BRING YOUR HISTORY WITH YOU
Save your session to Apple Health, and import your past Kilter and Tension logbook from Aurora Climbing.

FIND MORE TO CLIMB
Home brings together your week’s climbing, climbs at your level and projects to try again. Find nearby boards and your gym’s walls with location enabled. Filter Kilter’s Verified climbs, find your own climbs in Discover, and share playlists with your crew.

Supported boards: Kilter, Tension, MoonBoard (2010, 2016, 2024, Masters 2017, Masters 2019), Woods (12x12, 8x10), Decoy, Touchstone, Grasshopper and So iLL. Plus Spraywalls: any Spraywall you can photograph.

GPU-accelerated board rendering brings smoother climb transitions on supported boards and devices. Training apps can integrate with Boardsesh; developer details: https://www.boardsesh.com/developers/
