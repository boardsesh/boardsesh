New in 2.6:

YOUR GYM'S OTHER BOARDS, ONE TAP AWAY

A Kilter next to a Tension, or two walls of the same board at different angles? The wall sheet now lists the other boards at your gym, so you hop to the one you're standing at in one tap instead of scrolling past boards in other cities. The Bluetooth link moves with you, a queue built across two walls plays in order, and each board keeps its own name and angle.

A REST TIMER THAT MOVES THE QUEUE ON

Run boulders on the minute without picking your phone up between goes. Turn on the rest timer in the Record tab, set your rest, and it starts counting the moment you log a climb, then runs past zero into the red if you sit down too long. Let it move to the next climb and it lights that climb on the wall for you. "After each send" gives you the full rest every time; "On the minute" holds the beat however long the go takes.

ROUTES IN SECONDS, NOT MULTIPLIERS

Route pacing now reads in seconds a frame, the unit the setter authored in, and reaches 60 seconds a frame. Half the route catalogue was set slower than the old control could play.

FIXED

- Woods: every climb shows up whatever angle it was set at, carrying the grade it was given, and the setter filter lists all of a board's setters instead of the few with a climb graded at your angle.
- MoonBoard: a problem graded at only one of the two angles now shows an estimate for the other.
- Tap the lightbulb while a friend's phone holds the board and the climb lights over their link, instead of failing to connect.
- Swiping in the play drawer stays on the list you picked from, even after logging a climb reorders that list.
- Android: the Climbs tab no longer crashes when a board connects, offline saving keeps working after a long spell in the background, and switch labels are readable in dark mode.

Free, no ads, open source.
