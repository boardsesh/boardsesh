New in 2.8.0

Boardsesh is now available in Simplified Chinese.

MORE WAYS TO TRAIN

Build 4x4, Limit Bouldering and On the Minute workouts alongside Volume, Pyramid, Ladder and Grade Focus. Preview your climbs and rests before starting. Pyramids can now come back down on the same climbs. Choose countdown buzzes and whether rest countdowns appear on your Lock Screen in Settings > Timers.

PICK UP WHERE YOU LEFT OFF

Home brings together your week’s climbing, climbs at your level and projects to try again. See your last workout and start a session from the Session card. Customize which sections you see. The board picker brings nearby boards and your gym’s walls within reach when location is enabled. Find your own climbs in Discover.

Light your Spraywall

Connect your Spraywall to WLED over Wi-Fi. Map holes, LEDs and cable on a zoomable photo, test individual holds and save the setup for your crew. Find controllers on your Wi-Fi, adjust brightness and choose an automatic switch-off time for Wi-Fi lighting.

Compatible controllers with Boardsesh Bluetooth support can also light climbs over Bluetooth. Installing Bluetooth support remains an opt-in beta for matching controllers, with compatibility checks and separate consent.

FIND AND SHARE YOUR NEXT CLIMB

Kilter’s Verified climbs have their own quick filter beside Grade. Share playlists, profiles, sessions, gyms and boards with lit-climb link previews. Playlist headers put Share within reach.

EASIER TO READ, SMOOTHER TO BROWSE

GPU-accelerated board rendering brings smoother climb transitions on supported boards and devices.

Refreshed colours and layouts give your climbs more room. Text follows your iPhone’s text-size settings more closely, and VoiceOver announces the new climb after a swipe. Climb transitions respect Reduce Motion. Long climb names scroll again, and changing the grade filter keeps the Climbs list on screen.

CONNECT WITH TRAINING APPS

Open workouts from compatible third-party training apps in Boardsesh and let apps read your logbook with your permission. Developer details: https://www.boardsesh.com/developers/
