SPRAY WALLS: YOUR OWN WALL IN BOARDSESH

Photograph your home wall or the spray wall at your gym and the app finds the holds. Fix what it missed, pick a look, publish, and start setting. Climbs work like on any other board: queue them, save them to playlists, and log your sends in the same logbook as your other boards. No LEDs needed: the holds are highlighted on your photo.

Keep a wall private, make it public, or share it by link or QR code and your crew can set and log on it too. Stripped the wall? Reset it: take a new photo, mark the holds, and your old wall is archived with every climb and send. Swap a single hold and any climb that used it is flagged, ready to remix.

Free, like the rest of Boardsesh.

YOUR CREW, ON EVERY CLIMB

Home shows who's climbing right now, so you can jump into their session. Open a climb and the people you follow come first: their notes, the grade they'd give it, then everyone else's logs. Follow a setter and their new climbs land in your crew feed as one card you swipe through.

A REST TIMER THAT MOVES THE QUEUE ON

Run boulders on the minute without picking your phone up between goes. Turn on the rest timer in the Session tab and it starts counting the moment you log a climb, then runs past zero into the red if you sit down too long. Let it move to the next climb and it lights that climb on the wall for you. Someone wants a go first? Long-press their climb and tap Play next to slot it right behind the one on the wall.

FIXED

- Woods: every climb shows up whatever angle it was set at, and the setter filter lists all of a board's setters.
- MoonBoard: a problem graded at only one of the two angles now shows an estimate for the other.
