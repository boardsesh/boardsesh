SPRAY WALLS: YOUR WALL IN BOARDSESH

Add a photo of your home wall or your gym's spray wall, review the detected holds, and start setting boulders. Queue climbs, save them to playlists and log your sends. No LEDs needed.

Keep a wall private or share it with your crew by link or QR code. Reset your wall while keeping its old climbs and sends archived. Download a wall to keep its photo, holds and climbs available with no signal. On iPad, use Apple Pencil to mark holds.

YOUR CREW AND YOUR NEXT GO

Find your crew's live sessions on Home. Open a climb to see logs from people you follow alongside your own, and follow setters to see their new climbs in your crew feed.

Set a rest interval after each logged climb, or climb on a fixed timer. Turn on automatic queue advance and keep Boardsesh open between goes. Use Play next to put a climb straight after the one on the wall, or add a playlist without clearing your queue.

MORE WAYS TO MAKE IT YOURS

- Choose which sections appear below a climb, including beta videos, logs and similar climbs.
- Turn on Use my grades to browse, sort and filter by your own grades.
- Similar climbs and the hold heatmap work on your downloaded board with no signal.
- Switch to another board at your gym from the wall panel.
- Export your climbing history, playlists and climbs from Settings.
