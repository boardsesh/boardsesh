New in 2.8.0

LIGHT YOUR SPRAYWALL

Connect your Spraywall to WLED over Wi-Fi. Mark holes on a zoomable photo, lay out the LEDs and cable, and correct which LEDs light each hold. Test individual holds and see your Spraywall light up while you set a climb. Save the setup once so your crew can light climbs too.

Compatible controllers with Boardsesh Bluetooth support can also light climbs over Bluetooth. Owners can inspect and back up their controller from LED settings. Installing Bluetooth support is an opt-in beta for matching controllers; the app checks compatibility and asks for separate consent before installation.

FIND VERIFIED CLIMBS

Kilter's Verified climbs have their own quick filter beside Grade. Combine it with Liked or My drafts to narrow your list.

SHARE YOUR NEXT SESSION

Shared playlists, profiles, sessions, gyms and boards show a lit climb in link previews. Invite links show who you'd be climbing with and on which board. Playlist headers put Share within reach.

SMALL FIXES FOR YOUR NEXT GO

Long climb names scroll in the player again. The board angle stays visible in the Climbs bar, and the wall button's lights move with you as you pick the next climb.
