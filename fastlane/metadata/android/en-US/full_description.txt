One app for your boards. Light climbs, share a queue with your crew and keep your sends in one logbook.

Works with Kilter, Tension, MoonBoard, Woods, Decoy, Touchstone, Grasshopper and So iLL, plus your Spraywall. Connect to compatible controllers over Bluetooth to light the holds.

CLIMB WITH YOUR CREW
Start a session, invite your crew and share a queue everyone can control. Board history shows climbs lit through Boardsesh and recent sends on the physical board. End your session for a recap of your climbing.

TRAIN YOUR WAY
Build workouts with warm-ups, Volume, Pyramid, Ladder, Grade Focus, 4x4, Limit Bouldering or On the Minute. Preview climbs and rests, then work through them on the wall. Pyramids can repeat the same climbs on the way down.

Open workouts from compatible training apps in Boardsesh and let apps read your logbook with your permission. Developer details: https://www.boardsesh.com/developers/

Your Spraywall, too
Photograph your Spraywall, check the suggested holds and create climbs on the photo. Share it with your crew, queue climbs and log sends. No LEDs needed. Reset the Spraywall with a new photo while keeping its old climbs and sends archived.

With WLED and LEDs, map the lights on your photo and save the setup for your crew. Find controllers on Wi-Fi, set brightness and choose Wi-Fi auto-off. Compatible controllers with Boardsesh Bluetooth support can also light climbs over Bluetooth; installing that support is an opt-in beta for matching controllers.

LESS PHONE, MORE CLIMBING
A simpler Android interface and slimmer navigation keep climbs in focus. The lock-screen card puts the bulb and Next within reach; a ring shows timed driving. Set countdown buzzes and Lock Screen rest countdowns in Settings > Timers. GPU-accelerated rendering brings smoother climb transitions on supported boards and devices.

FIND YOUR NEXT CLIMB
Home shows your week, climbs at your level and projects to try again. Choose which sections you see. Find nearby boards with location enabled, filter Kilter’s Verified climbs, and find your own climbs in Discover.

TRACK YOUR PROGRESS
Log sends, flashes and attempts, follow your progress across boards, and import your Kilter and Tension history from Aurora Climbing.
