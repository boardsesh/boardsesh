## Attempts

The first `all` attempt stopped during the third board's preparation dwell at
14:38:32 UTC on 3 November 2026. The backend closed its evidence WebSocket with
code 1000 and reason `Server shutting down`. The preceding protocol pong had
matched 1.34 seconds earlier. The runner stopped rather than reconnecting across
an evidence gap. No matrix case completed and no crew session existed.

A subsequent readiness doctor passed before starting a fresh `all` attempt.
Results from an interrupted attempt are not combined into a complete run.

The second attempt completed all three preparation dwells, then the first case
boundary restart activated a downloaded production update. The runtime guard
stopped execution before the first physical send with `ios: running revision
changed during the suite`. Its new update was
`dd346ff4-4f15-a161-42fc-0dec09eee85d`, with the same native runtime. No crew
session existed. Startup revision settling is separate from matrix coverage.

The third attempt completed preparation and confirmed the first Moon Landing
physical send on Kilter with the iPhone. It then failed `return-current.yaml`: the
mini-player correctly showed the later preview, Angel Eyes, while the wall capsule
still showed Moon Landing. The harness expected the mini-player to name the wall
climb, contrary to the last-look interaction model. That initial send is partial
evidence, not a completed case. The other 17 cases were unrun.

A first targeted follow-up verified that closing the preview and opening the normal
untargeted player restores Moon Landing. Its Next step exposed two additional
harness assumptions: the current player uses swipe navigation instead of the old
Next-button selector, and raw hierarchy classification does not distinguish a timed drive from
an open-ended drive or retained hidden player state. The visible drive was
asserted before the old lookup; the later idle screenshot was consistent with
the default 45-second timer expiring. This diagnostic was not counted as a matrix case.
Normal owned Disconnect left all fixture links at zero and released the lease.

The second targeted follow-up passed Connect, queueing Angel Eyes, reopening the
committed player, deliberate open-ended Drive, one board swipe to Next, and normal
Disconnect. Moon Landing setup and Angel Eyes delivery had separate fresh decoded
commands on the same expected connection generation; Angel Eyes also had the
matching holder, subscription event and durable wall history. Unrelated boards
remained untouched. All links returned to zero and the lease was released. This
is a targeted diagnostic pass, not a full-case or full-matrix pass.

## Evidence and limits

Private artifacts stay under the local issue checkout's `.boardsesh/` directory.
They include device/account identifiers, runtime receipts, exact decoded USB
commands, observer events, native hierarchies and screenshots. Flash backups,
credentials and raw phone traces are excluded from this report and public
attachments.

Every claimed send requires a fresh exact decoded command, expected physical
connection generation and holder, live subscription event, and durable saved-wall
history. Preview isolation, driving frost, lifecycle and background behavior
require their own observed gates. Suspended-iOS cross-device wake remains a
separate known limitation tracked in #2174.
