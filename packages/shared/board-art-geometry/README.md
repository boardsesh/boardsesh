# @boardsesh/board-art-geometry

Every hold on every board, traced out of the board art and shipped as polygons
the renderer can fill, stroke or clip a glow to, plus the lightness readings
and LED positions the renderer cannot measure at draw time. Full documentation:
[`docs/board-art-geometry.md`](../../../docs/board-art-geometry.md).
